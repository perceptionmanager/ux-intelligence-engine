How this briefing is made

An automated pipeline runs every Monday. Nothing on this page is written by hand, and nothing is updated between runs.

1

Collect

A scheduled job reads the RSS feeds of the publications listed below and keeps articles from the last two weeks.

2

Group

Claude groups articles that address the same practical question. A topic needs at least two different publications to qualify.

3

Synthesize

Claude writes each briefing from article titles and excerpts only, not full text. Read it as a map to the articles, not a summary of them.

4

Check

Code rejects any briefing that cites an article outside its group. If fewer than three feeds respond, the run fails and the previous briefing stays up.
